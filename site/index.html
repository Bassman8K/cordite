02 // Campaign

Six chapters, one stolen weapon

A private army has taken LANTERN, a package that turns any guided weapon against whoever launched it. You are KESTREL. You go in with Merlin and Shrike, two teammates who cannot be killed, against soldiers who call to each other, take cover, flank and shout for a medic when they are hit. Every chapter is its own mission with its own place, weather and rules, and you can start from any one you have reached or pick up from the last checkpoint.

03 // Armory

{{WEAPONS}} weapons, and counting

Service pistols to anti-materiel rifles, crossbows to rotary cannon. Each one is entered by its real numbers (calibre, muzzle velocity, weight, barrel, rate of fire, feed) and the game works out recoil, handling, drop and damage from those.

  • Every round is a simulated projectile with travel time, drag, drop and penetration.
  • Inspect any weapon in 3D, compare the figures, then carry two guns, a blade and a throwable.
  • The catalogue is data, so it keeps growing. If something is missing, ask for it.

04 // Footprint

Built to stay out of the way

Very little is stored. Terrain, buildings, weapon models and soldiers are generated when a chapter loads and released when it ends. What ships as files is what code cannot fake: human voices, a spoken story, gunfire recorded from the real weapons, and photographs of concrete, brick, rock and sand.

{{GAME_SIZE}}Game data

Code, fonts, chapter art, recorded sound and photographed materials. This is everything the browser and phone versions download.

06 // Testers

Help break it

CORDITE is in open testing. The fastest route to a fix is a build, a few minutes of play and a short report.

1

Grab a launcher

The portable Windows build, the Linux AppImage, the macOS zip and the browser version all run without installing anything. Unfinished builds from every commit are attached to the Actions runs.

2

Turn on the tester tools

In the game, open Settings, then Tester: invulnerability, bottomless magazines, unlock every chapter, a performance readout and a button that completes the current objective.

3

Tell us what happened

Open a bug report. The form asks for the build, your device and the chapter, which is usually all it takes to reproduce a problem.
